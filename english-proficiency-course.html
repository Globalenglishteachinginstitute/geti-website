Develop English from A1 to C2

GETI supports English learning across the A1–C2 progression. Learners can strengthen communication, grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing, listening and speaking while working toward higher levels of independent and proficient English use.

A1–A2 Foundation

Build essential vocabulary, basic grammar and everyday communication.

B1–B2 Independent

Develop clearer communication, wider vocabulary and stronger reading and writing skills.

C1–C2 Advanced

Refine accurate, flexible and sophisticated English for academic, professional and international contexts.

English learning areas

  • Grammar and sentence structure
  • Vocabulary and functional English
  • Reading comprehension and writing
  • Listening, speaking and pronunciation
  • Academic and professional communication
  • Progress checks and proficiency assessment
