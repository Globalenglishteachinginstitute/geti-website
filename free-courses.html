Free Eligible Certificates

Certificates below 100 hours are free after the applicable examination is passed.

Certificate fee information: GETI states that certificates below 100 hours are free. This includes eligible 20, 30, 40, 50, 60 and 80-hour certificates. Certificates from 100 hours and above may have the fee displayed by GETI after the examination.

Frequently Asked Questions

What free courses can I find on GETI?

GETI provides introductory TEFL, TESOL and English learning pathways online.

Are all GETI certificates free?

No. Eligible certificates below 100 hours are free; higher-hour certificates can have a fee.

Can I study on mobile?

Yes. GETI is designed for phones, tablets and computers.

Where do I start?

Open GETI, create or sign in to a student account, then choose an available learning or examination pathway.
