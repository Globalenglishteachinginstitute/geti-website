About GETI

The first area should help visitors quickly understand what GETI offers, how the academic structure works and what students can expect before signing in.

Structured online learning with a clear academic pathway

GETI can organize its public academic presence around transparent institutional information, programme discovery, admissions, academic regulations and official document verification. This first area acts as the public face of the platform.

Visitors should be able to understand the difference between GETI Baccalaureat and GETI University, see the general progression from level to level, identify available faculties and know how to move from public information into a secure student account.

For a professional presentation, the first area should balance visual attractiveness and useful academic information: what GETI is, how the system works, what students can study and how records are managed.

Mission

Provide accessible, structured online learning with lessons, assessments, academic records and student support.

Vision

Develop a reliable digital academic environment with verifiable records and professional student services.

Values

Integrity, transparency, accessibility, academic discipline, support and continuous improvement.

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

How do I register?

Create an account, complete admission and registration, then select the available academic pathway.

Where can I see my subjects?

After sign-in, open GETI School and choose your level, filière, semester and subject.

How are grades calculated?

The academic system combines continuous assessment and exam results and records grades, credits, average and GPA.

How can I verify a diploma?

Use the public verification form with Diploma ID, Student ID or Name.

What happens if I fail an exam?

The system supports resit/rattrapage according to the applicable academic rules.

Where can I request support?

Students can use the Help Desk after sign-in; general inquiries can use the public contact details below.
