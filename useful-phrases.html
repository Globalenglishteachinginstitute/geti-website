Practical English expressions organized by everyday situations. Select Listen for browser-supported English speech.

Introducing yourself

Phrase 1: Hello, my name is Alex.

Phrase 2: Nice to meet you.

Meeting someone

Phrase 1: How are you doing?

Phrase 2: It is great to meet you.

Asking for help

Phrase 1: Could you help me, please?

Phrase 2: I would appreciate your assistance.

Making a request

Phrase 1: Could you please repeat that?

Phrase 2: Would you mind speaking more slowly?

Giving opinions

Phrase 1: In my opinion, this is useful.

Phrase 2: I think we should try another approach.

Agreeing and disagreeing

Phrase 1: I completely agree with you.

Phrase 2: I see your point, but I disagree.

At a restaurant

Phrase 1: Could I see the menu, please?

Phrase 2: May I have the bill, please?

At the airport

Phrase 1: Where is the check-in counter?

Phrase 2: Is this the gate for flight 204?

At work

Phrase 1: Could we arrange a short meeting?

Phrase 2: I will send you the report today.

Shopping

Phrase 1: How much does this cost?

Phrase 2: Do you have this in a different size?

On the phone

Phrase 1: May I speak to the manager?

Phrase 2: Could you call me back later?

Thanking and apologizing

Phrase 1: Thank you for your help.

Phrase 2: I am sorry for the inconvenience.

Asking for directions

Phrase 1: How do I get to the station?

Phrase 2: Is it within walking distance?

Making plans

Phrase 1: Are you free this weekend?

Phrase 2: Let us meet at three o’clock.

Talking about feelings

Phrase 1: I am excited about the opportunity.

Phrase 2: I feel a little nervous today.

Clarifying information

Phrase 1: What do you mean by that?

Phrase 2: Could you give me an example?
