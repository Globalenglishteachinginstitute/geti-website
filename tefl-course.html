What is the GETI TEFL pathway?

TEFL means Teaching English as a Foreign Language. The GETI TEFL pathway is designed for learners and teachers who want to develop knowledge of English teaching, classroom practice, lesson planning, language skills, assessment and professional teaching development.

Teaching knowledge

Study practical principles used when teaching English to learners in different learning contexts.

English skills

Connect grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing, listening, speaking and pronunciation with classroom practice.

Assessment pathway

Use GETI learning resources and continue to the relevant examination when you are ready to demonstrate your knowledge.

Learning outcomes

  • Understand core TEFL concepts and teaching terminology.
  • Plan clearer English lessons and learning activities.
  • Develop classroom-management and learner-support strategies.
  • Connect language knowledge with practical teaching decisions.
  • Prepare for GETI TEFL assessment and certificate procedures.
