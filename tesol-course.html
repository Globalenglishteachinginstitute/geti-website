TESOL learning with GETI

TESOL means Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages. GETI's TESOL pathway focuses on the knowledge and practical understanding needed to support learners who are developing English for study, communication, work and wider international use.

Learner-centred teaching

Develop ways to explain language clearly and adapt learning activities to different learner needs.

Communication skills

Connect speaking, listening, reading and writing with purposeful English communication.

Professional development

Build a foundation for ongoing English-teaching study, assessment and professional growth.

What learners can develop

  • Understanding of TESOL principles and terminology.
  • Lesson-planning and classroom communication skills.
  • Approaches for grammar, vocabulary and language-skill development.
  • Awareness of learner differences and teaching contexts.
  • Preparation for GETI TESOL assessment and certificate procedures.
