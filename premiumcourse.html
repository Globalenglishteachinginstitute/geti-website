Certification Through Achievement

GETI certificates are earned through achievement. Candidates must successfully meet the applicable learning and assessment requirements of their selected program. This approach helps ensure that certification reflects demonstrated knowledge, skills and successful completion.

Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about GETI Premium Courses, assessments and certification.

How do I start a GETI Premium Course?

Select your preferred training program, complete the applicable registration process and follow the instructions provided to begin your learning journey.

Can I access GETI courses on mobile?

GETI online services can be accessed using compatible devices. A stable internet connection and an updated browser are recommended.

How does GETI certification work?

Candidates must complete the applicable program requirements and successfully meet the required assessment standards before proceeding through the certification process.

Can my certificate be verified?

Where an official GETI certificate record is available, the corresponding certificate information can be checked through the GETI certificate verification system.

How can I contact GETI?

For assistance with registration, learning, examinations, results or certification, contact GETI Administration at admin@getienglish.com.
