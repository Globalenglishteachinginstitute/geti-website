GETI certificates and verification

GETI certificates are connected to the institute's learning and examination processes. Certificate details recorded in the GETI verification system can be checked online using the official certificate verification area.

TEFL & TESOL

Teaching-related certificate pathways include TEFL and TESOL programs and assessments available through GETI.

English proficiency

English assessment and proficiency pathways support learners working across GETI's English learning levels.

Professional subjects

GETI also provides examinations in a range of professional and skills-based subject areas shown on the main platform.

How to check authenticity

  1. Open the official GETI certificate verification page.
  2. Enter the certificate ID, or use the wider search options available on the main GETI verification interface.
  3. Review the certificate information returned by the official GETI verification system.
