ABOUT GETI

Building Skills for a Global Future

Global English Teaching Institute (GETI) is an English education and professional training platform dedicated to helping learners and teachers develop practical English language and teaching skills.

Our approach combines structured learning, practical communication, professional development and assessment.

  • A1–C2 English learning
  • TEFL professional education
  • Modern TESOL education
  • Online learning resources
  • English assessment
  • Certificate verification

GETI PROFESSIONAL EXAMINATION

TEFL EXAM

The GETI TEFL examination contains 10 subjects × 15 questions = 150 questions.

Each subject is completed separately. A result is displayed after every subject, followed by the next subject.

1. TEFL Foundations & Principles
2. English Grammar
3. Teaching Methodologies
4. Lesson Planning
5. Classroom Management
6. Teaching Listening
7. Teaching Speaking
8. Teaching Reading
9. Teaching Writing
10. Assessment & Testing
