ABOUT GETI

Building Skills for a Global Future

Global English Teaching Institute (GETI) is an English education and professional training platform dedicated to helping learners and teachers develop practical English language and teaching skills.

Our approach combines structured learning, practical communication, professional development and assessment to support learners from beginner to advanced levels.

  • A1–C2 English learning
  • Professional TEFL training
  • Modern TESOL education
  • Online learning resources
  • Certificate verification

TEFL ONLINE LEARNING

TEFL Course Lessons

Study all lessons before taking the 100-question TEFL final examination.

Lesson 1 — Introduction to TEFL

Learn what TEFL means, the role of a TEFL teacher, the difference between EFL and ESL, and the basic principles of English language teaching.

  • Meaning of TEFL
  • Role of the teacher
  • Role of the learner
  • EFL and ESL

Lesson 2 — Teaching Methodology

Study major approaches and methods used in English language teaching.

  • Grammar Translation
  • Direct Method
  • Audio-Lingual Method
  • Communicative Language Teaching

Lesson 3 — Lesson Planning

Learn how to create clear and effective English lessons.

  • Learning objectives
  • Lesson stages
  • Activities
  • Timing
  • Assessment

Lesson 4 — Classroom Management

Learn how teachers organize classroom activities, student behavior and learning environments.

  • Classroom rules
  • Grouping
  • Instructions
  • Time management

Lesson 5 — Teaching Grammar

Learn effective ways to teach English grammar through context, examples and practice.

  • Presentation
  • Practice
  • Production
  • Error correction

Lesson 6 — Teaching Vocabulary

Learn how to present, practice and recycle new English vocabulary.

  • Meaning
  • Pronunciation
  • Spelling
  • Collocations

Lesson 7 — Teaching Speaking

Learn how to create activities that develop fluency, accuracy and confidence.

  • Pair work
  • Role plays
  • Discussions
  • Information gaps

Lesson 8 — Teaching Listening

Learn how to prepare learners for authentic spoken English.

  • Pre-listening
  • While-listening
  • Post-listening
  • Listening strategies

Lesson 9 — Teaching Reading

Develop reading comprehension through effective pre-reading and reading activities.

  • Skimming
  • Scanning
  • Prediction
  • Comprehension

Lesson 10 — Teaching Writing

Learn how to teach paragraph writing, organization, accuracy and revision.

  • Brainstorming
  • Drafting
  • Editing
  • Feedback

Lesson 11 — Assessment

Learn how to measure learner progress using different assessment methods.

  • Formative assessment
  • Summative assessment
  • Testing
  • Feedback

Lesson 12 — Pronunciation

Study English sounds, stress, rhythm and intonation.

  • Vowels
  • Consonants
  • Word stress
  • Sentence stress

Lesson 13 — Online Teaching

Learn how to teach English effectively through online platforms.

  • Virtual classrooms
  • Online activities
  • Digital materials
  • Student engagement

Lesson 14 — Professional Development

Develop your skills as an English teacher and continue professional learning.

  • Teacher reflection
  • Professional goals
  • Continuous learning
  • Teaching portfolio

Lesson 15 — Learner Differences

Understand that learners have different needs, levels, backgrounds and learning preferences.

  • Age
  • Level
  • Motivation
  • Learning needs

Lesson 16 — Teaching Young Learners

Learn techniques for teaching children through games, songs, stories and movement.

  • Games
  • Songs
  • Stories
  • Repetition

Lesson 17 — Teaching Adults

Learn how to create practical and relevant lessons for adult English learners.

  • Professional needs
  • Real-life English
  • Discussion
  • Autonomous learning

Lesson 18 — Error Correction

Learn when and how to correct learner errors without reducing confidence.

  • Immediate correction
  • Delayed correction
  • Self-correction
  • Peer correction

Lesson 19 — Teaching Materials

Learn how to select and adapt materials for different English learners.

  • Textbooks
  • Worksheets
  • Videos
  • Authentic materials

Lesson 20 — Final TEFL Preparation

Review the major concepts before taking the 100-question final examination.

  • Review methodology
  • Review lesson planning
  • Review classroom management
  • Review assessment
