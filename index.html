ABOUT GETI

Building Skills for a Global Future

Global English Teaching Institute (GETI) is an English education and professional training platform dedicated to helping learners and teachers develop practical English language and teaching skills.

Our approach combines structured learning, practical communication, professional development and assessment to support learners from beginner to advanced levels.

  • A1–C2 English learning
  • Professional TEFL training
  • Modern TESOL education
  • Online learning resources
  • Certificate verification

TEFL Lessons

Complete these lessons before taking the final examination.

Lesson 1 — Introduction to TEFL

Meaning, purpose and role of TEFL.

Lesson 2 — Teaching Methodology

Major approaches and methods for teaching English.

Lesson 3 — Lesson Planning

Objectives, procedures, activities and timing.

Lesson 4 — Classroom Management

Managing students, behavior and learning environment.

Lesson 5 — Teaching Grammar

Practical ways to teach English grammar.

Lesson 6 — Teaching Vocabulary

Vocabulary presentation, practice and review.

Lesson 7 — Teaching Speaking

Communication activities and fluency development.

Lesson 8 — Teaching Listening

Listening strategies and classroom activities.

Lesson 9 — Teaching Reading

Reading strategies and comprehension skills.

Lesson 10 — Teaching Writing

Writing development, organization and feedback.

Lesson 11 — Assessment

Testing, evaluation and learner progress.

Lesson 12 — Pronunciation

Sounds, stress, rhythm and intelligibility.
