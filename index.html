ABOUT GETI

Building Skills for a Global Future

Global English Teaching Institute (GETI) is an English education and professional training platform designed to help learners and teachers develop practical English and teaching skills.

Our programs combine structured lessons, practice, assessment and professional development.

  • A1–C2 English learning
  • TEFL teacher training
  • TESOL training
  • Online learning
  • Certificate verification

TEFL PROGRAM

Lessons and final examination are available inside this TEFL section.

TEFL Online Lessons

Lesson 1 — Introduction to TEFL

Meaning, purpose and importance of Teaching English as a Foreign Language.

Lesson 2 — English Language Teaching

Basic principles of teaching English to language learners.

Lesson 3 — Learner Needs

Understanding learner goals, levels, needs and expectations.

Lesson 4 — Teaching Methodology

Major approaches and methods used in English language teaching.

Lesson 5 — Communicative Language Teaching

Using communication and interaction to develop language ability.

Lesson 6 — Lesson Planning

How to prepare objectives, procedures, activities and assessment.

Lesson 7 — Classroom Management

Managing students, classroom behavior, time and learning activities.

Lesson 8 — Teaching Grammar

Effective ways to explain and practice English grammar.

Lesson 9 — Teaching Vocabulary

Strategies for presenting, practicing and reviewing vocabulary.

Lesson 10 — Teaching Pronunciation

Helping learners improve sounds, stress, rhythm and intonation.

Lesson 11 — Teaching Speaking

Conversation, fluency, accuracy and communicative speaking activities.

Lesson 12 — Teaching Listening

Developing listening comprehension using authentic and graded materials.

Lesson 13 — Teaching Reading

Skimming, scanning, comprehension and reading strategies.

Lesson 14 — Teaching Writing

Paragraphs, essays, organization, grammar and writing development.

Lesson 15 — Teaching Young Learners

Teaching English through games, songs, stories and interactive activities.

Lesson 16 — Teaching Adults

Adult learner motivation, practical English and learner autonomy.

Lesson 17 — Assessment

Formative, summative, diagnostic and achievement assessment.

Lesson 18 — Error Correction

When and how teachers can correct learner errors effectively.

Lesson 19 — Online English Teaching

Using online platforms, digital materials and virtual classroom techniques.

Lesson 20 — Professional Development

Continuous learning, reflection, teaching portfolios and professional growth.
