Frequently Asked Questions

What is TEFL?

TEFL means Teaching English as a Foreign Language.

What is TESOL?

TESOL means Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages.

How is the TESOL exam scored?

The exam contains 50 questions. A score of 70% or higher is required to pass this demo exam system.

Yes. Enter the certificate ID in the Certificate Verification section.
