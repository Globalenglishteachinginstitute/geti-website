Your student account has been successfully opened. Before entering the GETI student website, please read the following important information carefully. These instructions explain what you should do during your studies, examination process, and certificate request.

Your GETI account is personal and is linked to your registered email address. Please keep your login information secure and do not share your password with another person. Use your own account whenever you access GETI services or communicate with the administration.

Important information about your certificate:

After completing your examination, carefully check your final result. If your examination status is PASSED, please send a message to the GETI Administrator through your student account. In your message, inform the administrator that you have successfully passed the examination and that you would like to receive your certificate.

The administrator will be able to review your account information and examination result before processing your certificate. Please make sure that the name and email address registered in your account are correct, because they may be used to identify your student record and certificate request.

  • Complete your examination using your own student account.
  • Check whether your final examination status is PASSED or FAILED.
  • If you PASS, send a message to the Administrator requesting your certificate.
  • Wait for the Administrator to review your examination result and certificate request.
  • Check your student account regularly for information or updates from GETI.
  • If you experience a problem, use the Message option to contact the Administrator.

By clicking NEXT, you confirm that you have read and understood these instructions. You will then enter the GETI student website.

ABOUT GETI

Building Skills for a Global Future

Global English Teaching Institute (GETI) is an English education and professional training platform dedicated to helping learners and teachers develop practical English language and teaching skills.

Our approach combines structured learning, practical communication, professional development, TEFL, TESOL and assessment.
