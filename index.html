About GETI

Global English Teaching Institute is focused on English language learning, teacher development and professional certification.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support learners and English teachers with accessible learning opportunities, practical teaching knowledge and professional development.

  • 🌍 International learning environment
  • 📚 English language development
  • 🎓 Teacher training
  • 📝 Professional assessment
  • 🏆 Certificate programs

Our Focus

GETI programs are designed to develop practical English teaching knowledge and communication skills.


Our TEFL program includes teaching methodology, lesson planning, classroom management, grammar, vocabulary, speaking, listening, reading, writing, assessment and professional development.
