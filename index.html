GLOBAL ENGLISH TEACHING INSTITUTE

Professional TEFL Examination System designed to evaluate English teaching knowledge, methodology and classroom skills.

TEFL Examination Subjects

10 subjects • 15 questions per subject • 12 minutes per subject

1. TEFL Foundations & Principles

Fundamental principles and concepts of Teaching English as a Foreign Language.

2. English Grammar

Grammar knowledge required by effective English teachers.

3. Teaching Methodologies

Major approaches and methods used in English language teaching.

4. Lesson Planning

Planning objectives, activities, materials and classroom procedures.

5. Classroom Management

Effective management of learners, activities, behavior and classroom time.

6. Teaching Listening

Principles and techniques for developing listening comprehension.

7. Teaching Speaking

Communicative activities and techniques for improving speaking skills.

8. Teaching Reading

Reading strategies, comprehension and classroom reading activities.

9. Teaching Writing

Writing instruction, correction, organization and assessment.

10. Assessment & Testing

Language testing, assessment methods, validity, reliability and feedback.

About GETI

Global English Teaching Institute (GETI) provides professional English language teaching and teacher training programs. The TEFL examination evaluates candidates across ten essential areas of English language teaching.
