Practice with a short, interactive English quiz. Choose an answer and select Check Answers to see your result.

1. She ___ English every day.

He, she, and it take -s or -es in Present Simple.

2. They ___ at school yesterday.

Were is the past form of be used with they.

3. I have ___ my homework.

Present perfect uses have/has + past participle.

4. If it rains, we ___ home.

First conditional uses if + present and will + base verb.

5. She does not ___ coffee.

After does not use the base form.

6. Which expression means “make people comfortable”?

Break the ice means ease social tension.

7. Please ___ the lights before you leave.

Turn off means switch off.

8. Which sound is often heard in American “water”?

American English often uses a flap [ɾ] in water.
